# Decentralized Education Cognitive Enhancement Platform

A blockchain-based platform for managing cognitive enhancement programs with transparent verification, safety monitoring, and research collaboration.

## Overview

This platform provides a comprehensive suite of smart contracts for managing cognitive enhancement education programs. It ensures institutional verification, protocol standardization, outcome measurement, safety monitoring, and research collaboration in a decentralized manner.

## Smart Contracts

### 1. Institution Verification Contract (`institution-verification.clar`)
- **Purpose**: Validates cognitive enhancement providers
- **Features**:
    - Institution registration and verification
    - Status management (pending, verified, suspended, revoked)
    - Credential tracking
    - Contact information management

### 2. Enhancement Protocol Contract (`enhancement-protocol.clar`)
- **Purpose**: Records cognitive enhancement methodologies
- **Features**:
    - Protocol creation and management
    - Version control for methodologies
    - Protocol categorization (memory, attention, processing, learning, creativity)
    - Duration and methodology tracking

### 3. Outcome Measurement Contract (`outcome-measurement.clar`)
- **Purpose**: Tracks cognitive enhancement effectiveness
- **Features**:
    - Participant enrollment
    - Pre/post/follow-up assessments
    - Multi-domain cognitive scoring
    - Improvement calculation
    - Statistical tracking

### 4. Safety Monitoring Contract (`safety-monitoring.clar`)
- **Purpose**: Ensures cognitive enhancement safety
- **Features**:
    - Safety event reporting
    - Severity classification
    - Safety officer authorization
    - Protocol suspension capabilities
    - Alert level management

### 5. Research Collaboration Contract (`research-collaboration.clar`)
- **Purpose**: Facilitates cognitive enhancement research
- **Features**:
    - Research project creation
    - Multi-institutional collaboration
    - Data sharing and publication tracking
    - Role-based access control
    - Funding management
